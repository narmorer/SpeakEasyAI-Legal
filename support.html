Sign-in and password help

Use the password recovery option on the sign-in screen. Follow the recovery email instructions. Check spam and junk folders if you do not see the message.

Microphone and practice sessions

Check that microphone access is enabled for SpeakEasy AI in your device Settings and that you have an internet connection. Try closing and reopening the app if recording or AI analysis stalls.

Subscriptions

See the Plans screen for available subscriptions. If you already purchased a plan, use Restore Purchases where available. Manage or cancel Apple-billed subscriptions at Apple Subscriptions.

Account deletion

Use Delete Account from the in-app Account screen when available. Deleting your account does not automatically cancel your App Store subscription. Contact support if the deletion process fails.
