Information you provide

SpeakEasy AI may process your email address, account information, recordings submitted for speech coaching, transcripts, practice answers, AI feedback, and progress history when you use the corresponding features. If you contact support, we process the information you include in your request.

How information is used

We use information to sign you in, deliver AI speech analysis and practice feedback, display and synchronize your practice history, respond to support requests, help secure the service, and maintain subscriptions.

Speech recordings and AI services

When you request speech analysis, the app sends the relevant audio or text to its backend and potentially to AI or speech-processing providers that perform the requested service. Do not submit sensitive personal information in practice recordings. Service providers and retention behavior must be confirmed before launch.

Storage and authentication

The app uses Supabase for authentication and may synchronize practice data to Supabase for signed-in users. Some guest data may remain on the device. Exact storage locations, security settings, backup procedures, and retention schedules must be verified against the production implementation.

Subscriptions

Purchases on iPhone are processed through Apple. SpeakEasy AI uses RevenueCat to help manage subscription entitlements. Payment-card information is generally handled by Apple rather than entered directly into the app.

Sharing

Information may be shared with the service providers needed to operate login, storage, AI processing, app hosting, and subscription verification, subject to their roles and applicable agreements. We do not claim a no-sharing or no-sale policy until the production data flows are audited.

Retention and deletion

You can request account deletion using the in-app account controls or by contacting support. Deletion may be subject to legally required retention and provider backup schedules. The actual deletion workflow and retention schedule must be verified before production release. Removing an account does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription.

Your choices

You may choose not to submit recordings, manage device microphone permissions, manage your Apple subscriptions, and ask about your information or deletion. Depending on applicable law, further rights may apply.

Children and international use

The app's minimum age, eligible regions, international processing practices, and any additional child-protection measures require confirmation before public launch.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the app or applicable requirements. For privacy questions, email support@justisend.com or visit our Help & Support page.
